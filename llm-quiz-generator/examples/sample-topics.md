## Tips for Best Results

### For People:
- **Be specific**: "Abraham Lincoln's presidency" vs. just "Abraham Lincoln"
- **Include context**: "Marie Curie's work on radioactivity"
- **Specify area**: "Leonardo da Vinci as an artist" or "Leonardo da Vinci as an inventor"

### For Events:
- **Include the name**: "The Battle of Gettysburg" not just "Gettysburg"
- **Add timeframe**: "The French Revolution of 1789"
- **Specify aspect**: "The causes of World War I" or "The aftermath of World War I"

### For Movements:
- **Be precise**: "The American Civil Rights Movement of the 1960s"
- **Include geography**: "The Italian Renaissance"
- **Specify domain**: "The Scientific Revolution"

## Testing Strategy

### 1. Start Simple
Try well-known topics first:
- "George Washington"
- "World War II"
- "The American Revolution"

### 2. Compare Models
Use the same topic with multiple models to see differences:
- GPT-4 often provides more detail
- Claude focuses on nuance
- Gemini is strong on facts

### 3. Test Edge Cases
- Controversial figures
- Complex events with multiple perspectives
- Recent history (20th/21st century)
- Non-Western history

### 4. Verify Accuracy
Always fact-check generated content, especially:
- Dates and timelines
- Causes and effects
- Key figures and roles
- Historical significance

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

### Too Vague
❌ "Napoleon"
✅ "Napoleon Bonaparte's exile to Elba"

### Too Broad
❌ "The Renaissance"
✅ "The Italian Renaissance and its impact on art"

### Too Specific/Obscure
❌ "The third sub-clause of the Treaty of Versailles"
✅ "The Treaty of Versailles"

### Ambiguous
❌ "Lincoln" (Abraham Lincoln? Abraham? The city?)
✅ "Abraham Lincoln, 16th President of the United States"
