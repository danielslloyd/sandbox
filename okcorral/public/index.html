    How it goes

    Seats in the ring are assigned at random and have nothing to do with where you are standing — sit wherever you like. Everyone hears every shot at the same instant, so the room gives away nothing. You only learn who fired by looking at them. That goes for the victim too. Town wins by killing the outlaws or by outlasting the clock, which is what forces the outlaws to make a move.
